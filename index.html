Welcome to TileBloom – Infinite Puzzle. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our game.

TileBloom does not require account registration or ask users to provide their name, phone number, or email address to play the game.

Gameplay information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own account registration system or cloud-based player profile service for this game.

Third-party advertising services may process information on their own systems according to their privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, analytics, security and related services, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

TileBloom is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising and consent handling must comply with applicable age-related and child protection requirements.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is cleared or the application is uninstalled. You can clear locally stored game data through your Android device settings.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their own retention and deletion policies.
